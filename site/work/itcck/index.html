A practical change to an everyday tool.

Email sits inside almost every part of a small team’s work. Moving it calls for attention to the existing setup, the people using it and the scope of the transition.

For ITCCK, the delivered project was specific: migrate four accounts from Cafe24 to Google Workspace. I carried out this client work through Zmantic.

Keep the scope concrete.

  • Starting point: four ITCCK email accounts on Cafe24.
  • Destination: Google Workspace.
  • Result: the four-account migration was completed.

The approach I bring to similar work.

Start by understanding the accounts and data involved. Define what the move includes, plan the transition, and check the everyday workflows people depend on. The exact checks and handover should follow the team’s needs and the agreed scope.

A small implementation deserves the same clarity as a larger product: a defined problem, a bounded piece of work and an outcome people can understand.
