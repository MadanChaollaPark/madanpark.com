A good hello needs somewhere to go.

A business card gives someone your details. CallMeMaybe gives them an easy way to say hello while you’re still talking. Their email lands in your inbox, ready for the next conversation.

Scan. Say hello. Keep in touch.

Add your receiving email and a short message, then create a QR you can share or print. When someone scans it, their configured email app opens a prefilled draft. They can edit it and tap Send.

The draft doesn’t send itself. The connection starts when their email reaches your inbox.

A tiny idea, taken all the way.

The project turns that simple interaction into something you can actually use: a custom email draft, a QR download, and a printable card. With a classic look or a playful “Girly mode,” even the introduction gets a bit of personality.

It’s the kind of side project I enjoy: take a familiar little ritual, ask “what if?”, and make something people can try.
