EUJobs: a focused place to find a career in European affairs. A venture I built and sold.

My roleFounder & product builder
FocusCareers in European affairs
OutcomeBuilt and sold

Start with a world you understand.

European affairs is a distinct professional community. A general job board can make it hard to find opportunities that fit. EUJobs brought that niche into focus.

Build around a real decision.

The product centered on helping people explore relevant roles and making opportunities easier to discover. It connected product decisions to a concrete, everyday need: finding the right next step.

See it through.

I built and sold EUJobs. It remains an important part of my story: taking an idea beyond a prototype and into a business outcome.

What I take forward.

Start with a community you can understand. Give a clear problem your full attention. Let that focus shape the product.
