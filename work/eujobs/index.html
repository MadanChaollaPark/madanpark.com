Market gap

Existing EU policy job boards often hide the data candidates care about: salary, deadline, source, employer type, work mode, seniority, and policy area.

Product surface

The prototype includes job search, filters, cards, detail panels, alert-ready flows, employer posting, source notes, and launch-roadmap pages.

Lesson

Niche markets reward specificity. A small job board can compete by making the hidden decision data explicit.
