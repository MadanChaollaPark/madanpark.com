Featured case study

Project KONGOR.

A multi-surface revival stack for a legacy game community: distributed services, launcher, documentation, archives, tooling, mods, and bot infrastructure.

Role

Systems/product builder across service topology, desktop UX, docs, and archive workflows.

  • .NET 10
  • Aspire
  • Blazor
  • Avalonia
  • Native AOT
  • Game infra

Problem

Legacy game communities need more than a nostalgic homepage. They need account services, reliable patching, launch flows, operator docs, mirrors, mod support, and tooling that works around old formats.

What shipped

`NEXUS` models the distributed service suite. `WILLOWMAKER` gives the client launcher and sync/update path. `CODEX` is the knowledge base. `ARCANA` is the archive/distribution toolchain. `MODS`, `BOTS`, and `GEMINI` cover legacy extension, AI behavior, and resource mirrors.

Why it matters

The project shows range: cloud service design, desktop application ergonomics, documentation, release integrity, and care for a community that depends on old technical assumptions.
